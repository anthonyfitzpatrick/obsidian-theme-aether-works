The second iteration of the engine replaces the `cast-iron` frame with a riveted brass chassis, after the manner of Mr. Babbage. See [[Brass Alloy Notes]] for the 70/30 composition that holds under a twelve pounds working load without *creep*.

## Of the Mechanism

- Eight columns of figure wheels, each *thirty-one digits* deep, keyed to the [[Camshaft]]
- [x] Cast the carry levers
- [ ] Fit the verdigris-treated bearings

> [!caution] Caution — Pressure
> "The engine does not think; it merely remembers with great precision."
> — margin note

```python
pressure = (steam_mass * R * T) / volume
if pressure > 12: vent_safety_valve()
```

## Order of Assembly

1. Mount the **frame** on the bedplate.
2. Seat the figure wheels, column by column.
3. Couple the crank and test by hand before any steam is raised.

| Alloy | Cu/Zn | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cartridge brass | 70/30 | 12 psi |
| Naval brass | 60/39 | 16 psi |

> Every column must be inspected by lamplight, and its readings entered in the ledger before the shift is out.
